
# FAST School of Management — Digital Rebranding & Promotional Strategy

### Building a stronger digital identity for a business school at the intersection of Business & Technology

## Project Overview

This Marketing Management project focused on developing a **digital rebranding and promotional strategy for FAST School of Management (FSM)**. The project examined FSM's existing market perception, digital presence, competitive position, and opportunities for stronger audience engagement.

The central idea was to understand how FSM could communicate its unique combination of **business education and technology** more effectively to prospective students in Lahore's competitive higher-education market.

Rather than focusing only on promotional content, the project used **market research, SWOT analysis, competitor benchmarking, segmentation, and positioning** to develop a strategy based on identified market gaps.

---

## The Marketing Challenge

A key challenge identified during the analysis was a gap between FSM's actual academic offering and how it was perceived in the market.

Although FSM had strengths such as:

* Qualified faculty
* Industry partnerships and MoUs
* Business programs incorporating technology
* Exposure to areas such as Data Analytics, FinTech, and SAP

the analysis suggested that these strengths were not being communicated strongly enough through its digital presence.

Another important perception issue was the association of **FAST NUCES primarily with Engineering and Computer Science**. This created an opportunity for FSM to communicate its identity more clearly as a school combining **business knowledge with technological skills**.

The marketing problem therefore centered on improving **visibility, communication, and positioning** rather than simply increasing the amount of promotional content.

---

## Situation & SWOT Analysis

A SWOT analysis was used to evaluate FSM's internal capabilities and external market environment.

### Strengths

* Skilled academic faculty
* Existing industry connections and MoUs
* Business programs with technology-oriented elements
* Strong association with the wider FAST NUCES brand

### Weaknesses

* Limited digital outreach
* Insufficient visibility of FSM-specific strengths
* Market perception strongly associated with FAST's technology and engineering programs

### Opportunities

* Growing importance of business and technology skills
* Increased reliance on digital platforms for university research and selection
* Opportunity to differentiate FSM through its business-technology combination
* Greater use of student-focused digital content

### Threats

* Strong competition among business schools in Lahore
* Established digital presence of competing universities
* Increasing competition for prospective students' attention across social platforms

The SWOT analysis helped connect FSM's internal strengths with opportunities that could be addressed through a more focused digital strategy.

---

## Competitor & Market Analysis

To understand FSM's position in the local education market, the project examined the digital communication approaches of competing institutions.

### Lahore School of Economics

The analysis looked at LSE's balance between **academic and extracurricular communication**. Its presentation style provided useful insight into how educational institutions can combine academic credibility with student-life content.

### University of Management & Technology

UMT's student-oriented communication, including interactive elements such as **Q&A and contest-based content**, was examined to understand approaches for increasing audience participation.

### COMSATS & ITU

The digital presence of COMSATS and ITU was reviewed at the department level, with attention to their technology-oriented communication, posting activity, visual presentation, and audience engagement.

Rather than copying competitor strategies, the benchmarking exercise was used to identify **communication gaps and opportunities for differentiation**.

---

## Target Audience & Market Segmentation

The promotional strategy focused primarily on **prospective students** who are evaluating universities and business programs.

The project emphasized the importance of communicating FSM's hybrid academic identity to students who are interested in both:

* Business and management
* Technology and data-driven skills
* Modern career opportunities
* Practical industry exposure

This positioning allows FSM to communicate beyond the traditional image of a business school by highlighting the connection between **business decision-making and technology**.

---

## Brand Positioning Strategy

A major part of the project was strengthening FSM's **Unique Selling Proposition (USP)**.

The proposed positioning centered around the idea of:

> **Business education enhanced by technology.**

Instead of presenting FSM simply as another business school, the strategy emphasized areas such as:

* Data Analytics
* FinTech
* SAP
* Technology-enabled business decision-making
* Business leadership

This approach was designed to make the school's academic identity more distinctive and address the perception gap identified during the research.
